Methodology

All source data was pulled from publicly accessible Reddit activity. No proprietary tools, no account access, no private data. The methodology is reproducible and follows standard open-source intelligence practices applied to social behavioral analysis.

  • Data collection. Full post and comment history scraped and archived. Activity timestamped and subreddit-mapped to identify thematic clusters and engagement patterns over time.
  • Thematic clustering. Posts grouped by subject matter — substance-adjacent communities, lifestyle indicators, risk-tolerance signals, and geographic/logistical disclosures. Cannabis-related or harm-reduction subreddits weighted heavily.
  • Linguistic analysis. Writing style profiled for consistency, emotional range, and coded language. Evasiveness, minimization, and implicit self-disclosure patterns extracted and scored.
  • Psychological profiling. Behavioral profile synthesized from communication style under conflict, responses to authority or challenge, and expressed value systems. Suitability for discreet engagement assessed.
  • Risk scoring. Final composite score produced across dimensions: acquisition likelihood, discretion reliability, escalation risk, and operational exposure risk.
